Nisk is often considered to be the last great city of [[Drazkuvar]]. It is by far the largest city across all of the islands and is the economic center of [[Ackriel Island]]. While the city is primarily populated by humans, it is the most diverse city in [[Drazkuvar]] and people of every species may be found there. 

Nisk is densely populated and can be divided in several ways. One of the most important ways is between the inner and outer city. The inner part of the city sits behind an ancient wall and holds the city palace, temples and other of the cities most important civic buildings. In addition, there is dense housing, inns, markets, shops, industrial centers and the city's most busy docks and quays.
